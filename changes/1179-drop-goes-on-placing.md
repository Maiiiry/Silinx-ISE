In short:

- **Placing symbols on a tablet:** a symbol dragged from the palette onto the sheet goes on being placed, a tap on the
  sheet placing another, until Select or Esc (it went back to Select after each one).

In detail:

- **A finger's drop keeps placing (#1179):** web/js/sch-mouse.js, on a touch screen (a coarse pointer); with the mouse,
  the symbol dropped is selected as before.
